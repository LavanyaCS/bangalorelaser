BANGALORE LAPAROSCOPY CENTER - META ADS LANDING PAGE
===================================================

FILES
- index.php: complete responsive landing page and appointment enquiry handler
- config.php: clinic phone, email, Google reviews link and Trustindex widget ID
- assets/style.css: responsive turquoise/blue/orange design
- assets/script.js: mobile navigation and 30-second consultation popup
- privacy-policy.php and terms.php: editable policy pages

UPLOAD
1. Download and extract the ZIP.
2. In Hostinger hPanel, open Files > File Manager.
3. Open the domain's public_html folder (or the campaign subfolder).
4. Upload all files and the assets folder, preserving the folder structure.
5. Open config.php and verify the phone, WhatsApp number, recipient email, sender email, profile review URL and widget ID.
6. Visit the landing page over HTTPS and test the form from a real external email address.

EMAIL FORM
The form uses PHP mail(). This requires PHP mail() to be enabled and correctly configured by the hosting provider. A successful mail() return means the server accepted the message for delivery; it does not guarantee inbox delivery. Check spam/junk and Hostinger mail settings. For reliable delivery, configure authenticated SMTP with a clinic mailbox and a maintained PHPMailer installation; do not put mailbox passwords in public files.

GOOGLE REVIEWS
No testimonials or ratings are fabricated in this template. To show live review cards, create a Trustindex widget for the exact Bangalore Laparoscopy Center Google Business Profile, then paste its widget ID into config.php. Alternatively, keep the Google profile link and let visitors open the live profile. Do not reuse a widget ID belonging to another clinic/profile.

IMPORTANT BEFORE GO-LIVE
- Confirm clinic phone number and WhatsApp number.
- Confirm the enquiry inbox and sender mailbox.
- Confirm the exact Google Business Profile, its review URL, rating and review widget.
- Verify both clinic addresses and whether both locations are currently active.
- Replace the DR placeholder in the hero with an approved doctor portrait.
- Verify doctor credentials, years of experience, services, claims and offer conditions.
- Have the Privacy Policy and Terms reviewed and approved by the clinic.
- Add Meta Pixel ID and conversion events only after the clinic supplies the correct Pixel ID and consent requirements are reviewed.

The page is a starter implementation. It does not include a guaranteed mail-delivery service, live review API, Meta Pixel ID, or verified medical claims.
